Our Story

Crafting connections over exceptional coffee since 2024

In the winter of 2024, two lifelong friends and coffee enthusiasts looked out at a busy street corner in Gqeberha and noticed something missing. The neighborhood had plenty of fast-paced storefronts, but it lacked a true gathering place—a warm spot where local university students, remote freelancers, and neighborhood residents could slow down and connect over a truly exceptional cup of coffee. Driven by a shared passion for artisan roasting and environmental sustainability, they opened the doors to The Daily Grind Café. The mission was simple: prove that premium specialty coffee and zero-waste, eco-friendly business practices could seamlessly exist under one roof.

Our Journey

The Daily Grind Café was founded in 2024 by two specialty coffee enthusiasts who wanted to bring sustainably sourced, organic coffee to the local community. What started as a small dream has grown into a bustling neighborhood hub where coffee lovers gather to connect, work, and relax.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve premium, ethically sourced specialty coffee and hand-crafted house pastries while building a warm, welcoming, and inclusive community space.

Our Vision

To become our region's leading eco-friendly coffee space and inspire green lifestyle changes by adopting zero-waste business standards.

Who We Protect

Sustainability runs through everything we do. From using entirely biodegradable, compostable takeaway cups to turning leftover ground espresso into organic plant food for local gardens, we work hard to keep our environment safe and healthy.
